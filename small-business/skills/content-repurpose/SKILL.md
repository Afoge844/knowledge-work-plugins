# Content Repurpose — Anchor-to-Asset Engine

Any finished content → full asset set for the week. This skill handles the mechanical transformation so Art's creative energy goes into the source content, not the derivatives.

## Step 1 — Identify the Source

Accept input in any of these forms:
1. **Pasted text** — essay, transcript, notes
2. **Notion URL** — fetch the document
3. **Google Drive / Docs link** — fetch the content
4. **Fireflies transcript** — pull from the last 7 days if Art says "use my last call" or "Fireflies"
5. **YouTube or video description** — fetch transcript if URL provided

Identify the content type: podcast episode / long-form video / newsletter essay / coaching call / interview / course lesson.

---

## Step 2 — Extract the Asset Material

Before building assets, pull:
- **The hook:** Best 1–2 sentence opener from the content
- **The core insight:** What's the single most valuable idea?
- **The story anchor:** Best personal narrative moment
- **3–5 quotable lines:** Pull quotes that stand alone without context
- **Tactical content:** Any step-by-step advice, tips, or frameworks mentioned
- **CTA:** What action does Art want the audience to take?

---

## Step 3 — Build Platform Assets

Build all assets in one pass. Scale the set to match the source content type:

**For a podcast episode or long-form video, build all 8:**

| # | Asset | Platform | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Short-form hook clips (3–5) | TikTok / Reels / Shorts | 30–90 sec script excerpts |
| 2 | LinkedIn long-form post | LinkedIn | 300–600 words, story-first |
| 3 | LinkedIn carousel outline | LinkedIn | 7–10 slides |
| 4 | Email broadcast | Rainmaker/GHL list | 4 paragraphs + subject lines |
| 5 | Substack Note series (7) | Substack | One per insight/quote |
| 6 | Skool community prompt | Skool | 1 discussion question |
| 7 | Facebook post | Facebook Page | 150–250 words |
| 8 | Blog article outline | Website/Notion | H2 structure + key points |

**For a newsletter essay, build assets 2–7 (use `/substack-prep` for the full 21-Notes version).**

**For a coaching call or Fireflies transcript, build assets 1, 2, 4, and 6.**

---

## Step 4 — Asset Specifications

### Short-Form Hook Clips (3–5 clips)
For each clip, provide:
- **Timecode or text excerpt** (the exact moment to clip)
- **Hook rewrite** (optimized opening line for social — hooks attention in 2 seconds)
- **Caption copy** (2–3 sentences + 1 CTA)
- **Platform fit** (TikTok / Reels / Shorts / LinkedIn)

### LinkedIn Long-Form Post
- Hook line → 3-line tension → "I was you" bridge → core insight → takeaway → CTA
- Voice: mentor, not guru. Conversational, direct. No hype.

### LinkedIn Carousel Outline
- Slide 1: Hook (bold statement)
- Slides 2–8: One insight per slide (headline + 1 sentence)
- Slide 9: Quote pull
- Slide 10: CTA
- Note: Art takes this into Gamma AI for visual production

### Email Broadcast
- 3 subject line options (curiosity / benefit / question)
- 4-paragraph body: hook → bridge → insight tease → CTA
- Warm, personal — not a marketing email

### Substack Notes (7)
- Mix: 2 insights · 2 story moments · 1 question · 1 quote · 1 soft CTA
- 1–4 sentences each. Standalone.

### Skool Community Prompt
- 1 framing sentence + 1 question + invitation to reply

### Facebook Post
- 150–250 words. Slightly more casual than LinkedIn. Story-first.

### Blog Article Outline
- Working title (3 options)
- H2 section headings (4–6)
- 2–3 bullet points per section
- Meta description (1 sentence)

## Step 5 — Voice Check Reminder

After delivering all assets:

> "These are ready for your voice checkpoint. Run through oJoy/Project Shepard before anything publishes. That's the non-negotiable quality gate."

Then offer:
- "Want me to save all assets to a Notion page organized by platform?"
- "Want me to put the social posts into a scheduling queue format?"

---

## Guardrails

- **Art's voice throughout.** Mentor, not guru. Conversational and warm. "I was you" framing where it fits naturally. No hype language.
- **Derive, don't invent.** Every asset must come from the source content. Do not introduce ideas not present in the original.
- **One content piece at a time.** Do not mix assets from two different source pieces in one session.
- **Voice checkpoint is mandatory.** Always remind Art at the end. Never suggest posting directly.
